# Flying

## Controls

Keys are physical keyboard positions (shown here for QWERTY).

| Keys      | Action                            |
| --------- | --------------------------------- |
| **W / S** | Pitch: lean forward / back        |
| **A / D** | Roll: lean left / right           |
| **Q / E** | Yaw: turn left / right            |
| **R / F** | Raise / lower the throttle (hold) |
| **Z / X** | Full throttle / cut               |
| **P**     | Start, pause, or resume           |
| **M**     | Mute or unmute sound              |
| **Enter** | Restart                           |

Drag the scene to look around (the camera returns behind the lander on its own)
and scroll to zoom.

### With a gamepad

A gamepad with the standard layout (Xbox, PlayStation, and most USB or Bluetooth
pads) works without setup: connect it and press a button. While you use it, the
**How to fly** card and Help show its controls.

| Gamepad         | Action                                                  |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Right stick** | Pitch and roll, in proportion to how far you push it    |
| **LB / RB**     | Yaw: turn left / right                                  |
| **RT / LT**     | Raise / lower the throttle: press harder to move faster |
| **A**           | Start, resume, or try again                             |
| **Start**       | Pause or resume                                         |

On a PlayStation pad, **A** is **✕**, **Start** is **Options**, **LB / RB** are
**L1 / R1**, and **LT / RT** are **L2 / R2**. Near the centre the stick responds
gently, for fine corrections; fully pushed it asks for the top turn rate. If the
pad disconnects mid-flight, the game pauses.

## The throttle is a lever

**R** and **F** move a lever: while held, throttle rises or falls by half its range
per second and stays where you let go. **Z** jumps to full and **X** cuts it at once.
A gamepad's triggers move it the same way, slower the lighter you press.

The engine pushes along the lander's axis. Upright, it slows your fall; leaning,
part of the thrust moves you sideways.

## Rotation is assisted

Rotation controls request a **turn rate**, not an angle (a stick, in proportion
to how far you push it). When you let go, attitude
control **stops** the rotation and the lander stays leaning where it was: it does
not level itself. Levelling out is your job.

Rotating **uses fuel** from the same tank as the engine: the attitude thrusters
burn propellant too.

## The camera

The camera follows from behind and turns with the lander, so **W always leans
toward the top of the screen** and **A toward the left**. It looks further down
when you are high, keeping the pad in view, and flattens near the ground.

## Sound

The engine rumbles with throttle, attitude thrusters hiss as you turn, the legs
thud on touchdown, and a short tone announces the result. All sound is synthesized
in the browser. **M** or the speaker button (**Mute**) silences it, and the game remembers
that choice.

## Pause

**P** pauses and resumes. The game also pauses when you open Help or switch tabs,
and stays paused until you press **P** again.

## Preferences

The game remembers in this browser your language, whether you muted sound,
whether you asked to **Reduce transparency**, and that you have read the **How to
fly** card (after your first flight it stops appearing; Help always lists the
controls).

**Reduce transparency**, in Help's **Display** section, swaps the translucent,
blurred panels for solid ones: easier to read and lighter for slow devices. It is
on anyway when your system asks for less transparency.
