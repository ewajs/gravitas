# Instruments

## Readouts

| Readout              | Meaning                                                        |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Altitude**         | Height of the lowest foot above the ground, in metres.         |
| **Vertical speed**   | Positive climbing, negative descending (m/s).                  |
| **Tilt**             | Angle between the lander's axis and vertical.                  |
| **Horizontal speed** | How fast you are moving sideways (m/s).                        |
| **To pad**           | Horizontal distance to the pad's centre (m).                   |
| **Fuel**             | Percentage of the tank. The first descent starts at 20%.       |
| **Throttle**         | Lever position. The white mark shows the throttle that hovers. |

## The landing radar

The radar faces where the lander points: **up is forward** (the W direction),
just like the camera.

- **Gold dot:** where thrust is leaning. The rim means 30°.
- **Blue arrow:** your horizontal drift. The rim means 10 m/s.
- **Diamond:** the pad. The rim is 60 m; beyond that it sits hollow on the rim,
  pointing the way.

To stop drifting, put the dot **on the opposite side from the arrow**: leaning
thrust brakes you. To move toward the pad, lean toward the diamond and brake
before you arrive.

## The hover mark

On the throttle bar, the white mark shows how much throttle holds the lander's
weight while upright. Above it you climb; below it you sink. As fuel burns the
lander gets lighter and the mark moves down.

## On the ground

A blue ring and line mark the ground point straight below the lander, helping
judge height and drift from far away.
