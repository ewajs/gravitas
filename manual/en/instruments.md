# Instruments

## Readouts

| Readout              | Meaning                                                                       |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Altitude**         | Height of the lowest foot above the ground, in metres.                        |
| **Vertical speed**   | Positive climbing, negative descending (m/s). The safe-sink scale sits below. |
| **Tilt**             | Angle between the lander's axis and vertical.                                 |
| **Horizontal speed** | How fast you are moving sideways (m/s).                                       |
| **To pad**           | Horizontal distance to the pad's centre (m).                                  |
| **Fuel**             | Percentage of the tank. The first descent starts at 20%.                      |
| **Throttle**         | Lever position. The white mark shows the throttle that hovers.                |

On a phone held sideways the readouts share one small card at the top left:
altitude and vertical speed large, the safe-sink scale as a thin bar, and one line
with short labels: **H** (horizontal speed), **Tilt**, and **Fuel**. The radar
card at the top right shows the distance to the pad under the radar, and the lever
on the left shows the throttle.

## The landing radar

The radar faces where the lander points: **up is forward** (the W direction),
just like the camera.

- **White dot:** where thrust is leaning. The rim means 30°.
- **Light-blue arrow:** your horizontal drift. The rim means 10 m/s.
- **Cyan diamond:** the pad, in the same colour as its ring on the ground. The rim
  is 60 m; beyond that it sits hollow on the rim, pointing the way.

To stop drifting, put the dot **on the opposite side from the arrow**: leaning
thrust brakes you. To move toward the pad, lean toward the diamond and brake
before you arrive.

## Safe sink

Under vertical speed, a scale from 0 to 10 m/s shades in green how fast it is wise
to sink at your altitude: up to an eighth of the altitude per second (10 m/s at
80 m, 5 m/s at 40 m), and never more than 2 m/s in the last 16 m. The tick shows
your current sink. Sink faster and the tick and vertical speed turn amber, with a
warning triangle. It is a guide: from that rate, full throttle stops you with height
to spare. The landing limits do not change.

## The hover mark

On the throttle bar, the white mark shows how much throttle holds the lander's
weight while upright. Above it you climb; below it you sink. As fuel burns the
lander gets lighter and the mark moves down.

## On the ground

A light ring and line mark the ground point straight below the lander, helping
judge height and drift from far away. The cyan ring with tick marks is the pad.
